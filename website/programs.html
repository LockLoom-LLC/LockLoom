Free & Discounted Licensing

LockLoom believes personal safety shouldn't have a paywall. We partner with advocacy organizations to distribute free Shield-tier licenses to those who need protection. (Expected 2027)

Our Principles

Every program below grants a full Shield-tier license — comprehensive protection — at no cost. All licenses are distributed through vetted advocacy partner organizations. We do not accept direct license requests. We never log or track program participants. We never share referral data with anyone.

While App Stores limit the number of promotional codes we can generate each quarter, our partner organizations can accept donations to procure additional licenses if those limits are reached.

Survivor Support Program Expected 2027

Full Shield-tier licenses for domestic violence survivors. We partner with advocacy organizations to distribute licenses — we do not accept direct requests. Partner organizations vet and distribute licenses on our behalf.

We never require survivors to prove their circumstances to us. That responsibility belongs to the distributing organization, and we encourage dignity-preserving, trust-based enrollment.

  • Full Shield-tier access — all core security features included
  • Distributed through vetted advocacy partner organizations only
  • No tracking, no logging of program participation
  • Lifetime license — no renewals, no expiration
