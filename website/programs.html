LockLoom believes personal safety shouldn't have a paywall. We offer free Guardian-tier licenses to those who need protection most.

Our Principles

Every program below grants a full Guardian-tier license — the highest protection level — at no cost. We never require survivors to prove their circumstances. We never log or track program participants. We never share referral data with anyone.

Survivor Support Program Free

Full Guardian-tier licenses for domestic violence survivors. We partner with advocacy organizations to distribute licenses — but we also accept direct requests with no personal verification required.

This is a trust-based, dignity-preserving program. We will never ask you to prove your situation.

  • Full Guardian-tier access (all features, all tiers unlocked)
  • No verification of DV status — trust-based enrollment
  • Available through partner organizations or direct request
  • No tracking, no logging of program participation
  • Lifetime license — no renewals, no expiration
