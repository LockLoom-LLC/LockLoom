How to Provision

LockLoom uses Android's Device Owner mode for the strongest possible device protection. This requires enrolling during the initial device setup (after factory reset).

Step 1

Factory reset your Android device (or use a brand new device).

Step 2

On the "Welcome" screen, tap the word "Welcome" 6-7 times rapidly.

Step 3

A QR code scanner will appear. Scan the QR code shown on this page.

Step 4

Android will automatically download, install, and configure LockLoom as Device Owner.
